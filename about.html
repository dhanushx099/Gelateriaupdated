Since 2004

Two decades of slow gelato

At Gelateria Cremona, our philosophy of maestria artigianale is rooted in a passion for authentic gelato making. Using premium ingredients and handpicked seasonal fruits, we create small-batch gelato made fresh daily – from timeless Italian classics to refined seasonal creations.

Tradition

Authentic Italian Tradition

Rooted in the time-honoured craft of Italy's finest gelaterias, our recipes stay true to the methods passed down through generations. Every flavour is built on authentic technique and quality ingredients, bringing the true taste of Italian gelato-making to every scoop.

Every step honours the artisans of Cremona who came before us. It's a tradition of care, not shortcuts — the same slow, hands-on process that has defined true Italian gelato for generations, brought to life fresh in our laboratory every day.

EFFe

Our traditional Effe machine, crafted in Italy, is at the heart of our gelato laboratory. While the process is slow and time-intensive, it allows us to create authentic small-batch gelato with exceptional texture, richness, and flavour — and the taste speaks for itself.

POZZETTI

Our traditional pozzetti preserve gelato at the ideal temperature, protecting its texture, flavour, and freshness. Unlike open display cabinets, this authentic Italian method shields the gelato from air and light — ensuring every scoop is served exactly as it should be.
